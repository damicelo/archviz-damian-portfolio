ABOUT

Damijan Koprivc

Architectural Visualization & Concept Artist based in Slovenia.

My work focuses on architectural visualization, concept development and presentation imagery for residential, hospitality, tourism and real-estate projects.

I work across scales — from interiors and individual houses to villas, mobile homes, glamping concepts, campsites, resorts and masterplans. The goal is to make a project understandable, desirable and visually convincing while preserving the architectural idea.

Services include photoreal architectural visualization, resort and campsite masterplan visualization, glamping concepts, interior visualization and real-estate presentation imagery for remote collaboration.
