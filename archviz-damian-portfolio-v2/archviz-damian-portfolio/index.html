About

Damijan Koprivc

Architectural Visualization & Concept Artist based in Slovenia.

My work focuses on architectural visualization, concept development and presentation imagery for residential, hospitality, tourism and real-estate projects.

I work across scales — from interiors and individual houses to villas, mobile homes, glamping concepts, campsites, resorts and masterplans. The approach combines architectural clarity with realistic materials, lighting, landscape and atmosphere.

The goal is to make a project understandable, desirable and visually convincing while preserving the architectural idea.

Process

From material to visual story.

A flexible workflow adapted to the material already available — plans, sketches, reference images, an existing 3D model or an early concept.

01

Project material

Plans, sketches, references, models and the intended presentation goal.

02

3D / Concept

Preparation, modeling or development of the scene and architectural concept.

03

Visualization

Materials, lighting, landscape, atmosphere and photoreal presentation.

04

Presentation

High-resolution imagery, project boards and optional short-form animation.

Architecture in Motion

Still images are only part of the story.

Short architectural animations and cinematic sequences can add movement, atmosphere and a stronger sense of place to a presentation.
