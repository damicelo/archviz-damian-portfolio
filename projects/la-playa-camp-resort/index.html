La Playa Camp Resort

Masterplan visualization · Architectural CGI · Investor presentation

From coastal site to a complete resort vision.

La Playa is a proposed 36-unit camp resort shaped by its direct relationship with the Adriatic coast. The visual study brings together the masterplan, internal circulation, mobile-home plots, Mediterranean landscape, beach access and planned marina connection into one coherent destination. Aerial views explain the complete development, while guest-level imagery communicates the atmosphere, accommodation and arrival experience.
